Brand identity guidelines

Contents

MXDE is pronounced Mode.

The X takes the place of the O. It stands for the variable: whichever mode you switch into after your coffee. Everything in this identity follows from that one swap.

These guidelines cover the logo, colour, type and the mode system. Packaging artwork, dielines, the website, photography and campaign work follow once the identity is signed off.

Items marked to confirm are working assumptions from the brief, waiting on the brand team.

MXDE brand guidelines · v1.0 · 02

The idea

01

Start from the word you want people to say, change one letter, and let that letter carry the brand. The round O becomes a square slot with an X cut into it. The slot keeps the O's place and weight, so the eye reads Mode. The X inside it spells MXDE and stands for the variable.

MXDE brand guidelines · v1.0 · 03

Wordmark

02

Built from straight lines

Every edge runs at 0°, 45°, 60° or 90°. There are no curves in the mark. The D, the only letter with a bowl, is drawn with two 45° chamfers, so it stays a D without a round shape.

The slot is the hero

The slot is the only solid shape in the word. At small sizes it reads as a dark O, so the name reads Mode. Up close, the cut shows the X. In full colour, the slot carries the accent and M, D and E stay Carbon.

One weight

Stems are 18% of the cap height. Lighter weights lost the slot's mass against the letters, and heavier ones closed the D's counter at 16 px. The cut is 62% of a stem, wide enough for foil and emboss.

Rules

Body copy, ingredients and nutrition panels: Carbon or Graphite on White, Paper or Oat only.

Stone is for captions of 18 px / 14 pt bold and above, or for rules and dividers. It isn't for body text on light grounds.

Mode colours pass 3:1 on Paper, so they work as tiles and large type. White text on a mode colour passes AA body (4.5:1), which makes buttons and chips safe. Don't set small mode-coloured text on Paper.

Never rely on colour alone. Every mode tile carries its own cut, and every mode is named in words.

Small-pack legibility

Mandatory label text has legal minimums. For example, EU 1169/2011 requires a 1.2 mm x-height, or 0.9 mm on packs whose largest face is under 80 cm². Plex Sans Condensed at 6 pt gives about 1.1 mm, so check the rules in each market you sell into (to confirm).

Mode system

11

Every mode is the same square slot, cut differently. The cut is the mode.

The X's two cuts are rearranged into a new sign for each mode. All cuts share one width (11.2 on a 100 square, 15% when the tile is drawn small), so the family stays one system. Each mode also has a tint. The tint helps, but the cut alone is enough. Every mode stays distinct in one-colour print, in greyscale and for colour-blind customers.

Zen and Calm. Zen's cuts stand upright: a mind that's aligned and focused. Calm's cuts lie down: a body at rest. They are perpendicular, and their tints are cool slate against warm clay.

MXDE brand guidelines · v1.0 · 13

Mode lockups

12

Fixed relationship

The mode line hangs from the X. The tile's left edge aligns with the slot's left edge. The tile and the mode name's cap height are both 32% of the wordmark's cap height. The line sits 36% of the cap height below the baseline, and the name starts 32% of a tile after the tile.

In mode lockups, the slot takes the mode's tint, so the X switches into the mode. M, D and E never change colour.

Names of up to six letters end inside the E. Longer future names may run past the E but keep every other measurement.

Switching in, in motion only

In animation and social content, the slot can morph into a mode's tile and then return to the X. Never set a mode tile inside the wordmark on packs, the site header or anywhere the name has to be read. A slot showing the WAKE ▲ reads as "MADE".

MXDE brand guidelines · v1.0 · 14

Growing the system

13

A sixth or seventh mode needs no redesign: draw a new cut in the same square.

  1. Use two straight cuts at the system's cut width, at 0°, 45°, 90° or along the square's diagonals and midpoints.
  2. The new sign must differ from all existing ones in silhouette at 24 px, in one colour. Test it in the thumbnail grid.
  3. Avoid cuts that turn the slot into a letter (▲ = A, ≡ = E) if the tile could ever sit beside the wordmark.
  4. Choose a tint from the blue–green–warm-neutral families with at least 4.5:1 for white text and 3:1 against Paper. Keep it clearly apart from its nearest neighbour.
  5. Name it in one word, ideally six letters or fewer.
