MXDE · logo and brand identity · proposal

October 2026

MXDE is pronounced Mode. The X is the letter you switch.

The slot stands in for the O. Pick a mode to switch it.

Functional coffee in single-serve sachets. One brand, many modes. This page tells the identity's story, from the word to the system, and shows it on the places customers will meet it.

The story in six frames

One letter changes, and the brand lives in that letter.

Told the way a logo-process reel is told: start with the plain word, select the letter that matters, change it, then reveal the mark.

Stage 2 · Three routes, black on white

Three ways to make the X the hero.

All three keep M, D and E straight and chamfered. They differ only in how the X carries "switch". Each was judged on whether it reads "Mode" before "M-X-D-E".

Route A

Crossover

One bar of the X passes over the other: a crossing point.

Reads as an X, but the break pulls the eye to M-X-D-E, and the gap fills in below 24 px.

Route B · recommended

Slot

The O becomes a square slot with the X cut into it. It reads Mode and spells MXDE.

The only route that reads 'Mode' first. At 16 px the slot is a dark O. Up close it's an X.

Route C

Shift

The X splits at its waist and the top half slides over, like a switch being thrown.

Has momentum, but the jog reads as a glitch and does nothing for 'Mode'.

Stage 3 · Refinement

Why it reads "Mode", even at 16 px.

The slot is the only solid shape in the word. Small or blurred, it reads as a heavy O between M and DE. Up close, the cut spells the X.

As drawn

Squint test: blurred

Every edge runs at 0°, 45°, 60° or 90°. The D is the only letter with a bowl, so it's drawn with two 45° chamfers, which keep it a D without a single curve. The M's V meets the baseline with a flat foot, so no point fills in when the mark is embossed.

The weight was chosen from a study at 10, 14, 18 and 22. At 14 and below the slot outweighs the letters. At 22 the D's counter closes at 16 px.

Mode sub-identity

Every mode is the same square, cut differently.

The X's two cuts are rearranged into a new sign for each mode. All cuts share one width and one square, so the family stays one system. Each mode also has a tint, but the cut alone tells it apart, in one colour and for colour-blind customers.

Zen and Calm, kept apart

Zen's cuts stand upright: a mind that's aligned and focused. Calm's cuts lie down: a body at rest. They're perpendicular, and their tints are cool slate against warm clay.

The mode line hangs from the X

In a mode lockup, the tile aligns with the slot, the name follows at the same height, and the slot takes the mode's tint. M, D and E never change colour.

Room for a sixth and seventh mode

  1. Two straight cuts, at the system's cut width, in the same square.
  2. Must differ from every existing sign in silhouette at 24 px, in one colour.
  3. Avoid cuts that make the slot read as a letter (▲ reads A, ≡ reads E) wherever the tile sits next to the name.
  4. A tint from the blue, green or warm-neutral families, with 4.5:1 for white text.

Colour

Neutrals do the work. Colour marks the mode.

Grays and warm neutrals carry the brand. One accent, Tonic, a mineral green, adds freshness. Mode tints appear only where a mode is named. Keep accent and tint to about a tenth of any layout.

Open questions for the brand team

  • Who is the target customer, and what is the price positioning?
  • What are the sachet dimensions and finish: matte, foil or kraft?
  • What are the exact function and time of day for each mode?
  • Which brands should we admire, and which should we avoid resembling?
  • SwitchYourMode or #SwitchYourMode? Proposed: no # in lockups, # on social and packs.
  • Has a trademark check been done on the name? The slot mark needs one too.
  • What are the timeline and design budget?

What to judge

  • Does it read as "Mode" at first glance?
  • Is it built from straight, angular lines with minimal curves?
  • Does it hold up at small sizes and in one colour?
  • Does the X carry the switch idea without feeling forced?
  • Does it feel premium and healthy, not like a café or an energy drink?
  • Can the five modes be told apart instantly?
